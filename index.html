Help and privacy

Rage Tracker Sync sends today’s steps, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and exercise minutes, last night’s sleep, and the titles, times and calendar names of today’s calendar events. Anything with no data today is left out.

Your device key is saved as a plain-text file, rage-tracker-device-key.txt, in a Rage Tracker folder in On My iPhone. It isn’t encrypted by Rage Tracker. Anyone who can open that file, and any Shortcut you let read it, can use the key. Don’t share it.

If Shortcuts asks, turn on Allow Sharing Large Amounts of Data in Settings → Apps → Shortcuts → Advanced.

To sync on a schedule, add a Time of Day automation in Shortcuts that runs Rage Tracker Sync, and choose Run Immediately. Your iPhone must be unlocked to read Health data.

To disconnect, revoke the key below and delete the file in Files.

Privacy

Mood check-ins stay on this iPhone. Steps and calendar events you sync are sent over HTTPS and stored in your private account. They aren’t end-to-end encrypted. You can revoke a device or erase synced data at any time.
