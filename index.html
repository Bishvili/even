Updates, permissions and other data

Shortcuts installed from iCloud do not automatically replace themselves when a new version is shared. Rage Tracker will always show the latest published link. Future Shortcuts can report their version so this screen can flag an update; installing it still needs your approval.

If you share a Shortcut after entering your Authorization value, anyone with its iCloud link may receive your private credential. Revoke that device key and share a fresh, unconfigured master instead.

The current shared release handles calendar events and steps. The server also supports sleep duration, HRV, resting heart rate and workout minutes; we'll add those to a future verified Shortcut release rather than infer their values from untested Apple Health actions.

Apple may require Settings → Apps → Shortcuts → Advanced → Allow Sharing Large Amounts of Data. Leave the deletion permissions disabled; this Shortcut should not delete your personal data.

Privacy and control

Mood check-ins remain on this phone. Selected Health measurements and Calendar data are sent over HTTPS and stored in owner-specific Cloudflare database records. Access uses email one-time PIN; device keys are revocable. Data is not end-to-end encrypted. Back up your local data before switching accounts.
