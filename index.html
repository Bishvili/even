Help and privacy

{{sync}} sends what you leave on under What {{sync}} sends: up to the last 7 days of steps, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and exercise minutes; your sleep from recent nights, or up to a month on the first sync; your alarms; and the titles, times and calendar names of events for today and the next 7 days from the calendars you keep. Anything with no data is left out, and anything turned off that still arrives is discarded.

Your device key is saved as a plain-text file, even-device-key.txt, in an {{keyFolder}} folder in On My iPhone. It isn’t encrypted by {{name}}. Anyone who can open that file, and any Shortcut you let read it, can use the key. Don’t share it.

If Shortcuts asks, turn on Allow Sharing Large Amounts of Data in Settings → Apps → Shortcuts → Advanced.

To sync on a schedule, add a Time of Day automation in Shortcuts that runs {{sync}}, and choose Run Immediately. Your iPhone must be unlocked to read Health data.

To disconnect, tap Disconnect this iPhone, then delete the file in Files.

Privacy

Your first name can appear in notifications, including on your Lock Screen. When you’re signed in, check-ins and their notes are saved to your private account so they appear on your other devices. They’re stored as readable text. Health data, calendar events and alarms you sync are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in your private account. None of this is end-to-end encrypted. Weekly reflections, breathing sessions, suggestions you mark done and which check-ins you answered stay on this device. Sign-in codes are sent through our email provider. If you use Google, it tells us your email and name. You can disconnect a device or erase synced data at any time. {{name}} is for the US and in English for now: support numbers are US only, and {{sync}} reads Health in English. Send feedback in Settings opens an email with your app and sync details, and nothing else, and sends only if you send it.

How the Shortcuts work

{{sync}} is what everyone installs. It sends Health and calendar data. Running it from this app connects it, so there’s no key to copy.

{{helper}} is for developers. It holds no credentials, and only approved accounts can publish.

To release a change: push it, then tap Run Release Helper. It keeps itself up to date, runs {{sync}} and checks the versions. If an update is needed, tap Replace and run it again. If a version needs publishing, it asks you first and tells you when it’s live.
